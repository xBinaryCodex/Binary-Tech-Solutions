Law Firm Security

Why Law Firms
Are A Target

Attorneys handle sensitive client data, financial records, and privileged communications — making them one of the most valuable targets for cybercriminals. Yet most small firms operate with consumer-grade security and no formal policies in place.

A single breach can mean regulatory fines, malpractice exposure, and irreparable damage to client trust. ABA Rule 1.6 requires lawyers to make "reasonable efforts" to prevent unauthorized disclosure — but "reasonable" is increasingly defined by what a professional security review would catch.
